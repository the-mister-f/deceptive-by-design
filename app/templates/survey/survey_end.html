Researcher & Study Information

This study is part of academic research conducted at Hochschule für Wirtschaft und Recht Berlin.
Study Title: Deceptive by Design: Assessing the Impact of UX Dark Patterns on Engagement and User Trust in Digital Products
Researcher: Firas Najar
Contact: s_najar20@stud.hwr-berlin.de

Understanding Dark Patterns

The purpose of this study was to examine how users interact with different interface elements and how subtle manipulation techniques —often referred to as dark patterns—can influence decision-making.
By participating, you have helped us gain deeper insights into how these techniques impact user experience, engagement, and trust.
